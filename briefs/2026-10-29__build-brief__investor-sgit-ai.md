# investor.sgit.ai: a build brief for the agent that makes it

*29 October 2026, written by newsroom.writer from Dinis Cruz's two voice memos of 28 October
(`2026-10-28__investor-sgit-ai/memo-01.md`, `memo-02.md`). The article is
[investor.sgit.ai](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/investor-sgit-ai.html). Status: a brief; nothing is built.*

## The ask
A website for investors and founders, angel to Series A, at investor.sgit.ai, that uses sgit vaults, PKI, agent
teams and RiskMandate in its own working, so that visitors experience the technology rather than read about it.

## Start the repository to the house rules (new agents miss this)
- sgit.ai's [Every sgit repository](https://sgit.ai/docs/guidance/repositories.md): the brief published in full,
  a reality file, BRIEF-CORRECTIONS.md, a gate, tests against real things, a review/ folder, roles and a board as
  files, a gitignored credentials tier with a leak tripwire, `.md` twins and llms.txt, Apache-2.0 and CC BY 4.0.
- **CI owns the version** (this newsroom's `admin/notes/2026-10-27__ci-owns-the-version.md` and the article
  [CI owns the version](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/ci-owns-the-version.html)): the pipeline picks the number
  (the last on `dev`, the middle on `main`), never an agent.
- Point to coding.sgit.ai, nfrs.sgit.ai and ux.sgit.ai; add the site's row to nfrs.sgit.ai's honest column.
- Every agent under an Agent Behaviour Policy; each session's key published with its role
  ([agents have an identity now](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/agents-have-an-identity-now.html)).

## Sections (first version: the first three, then the rest)
1. The news: angel and Series A, public sources only, dated and linked, as a graph. Open question for Dinis: the
   newsroom runs an investor wire (a public vault) that this site reads, or the site's own team runs it. The writer's
   recommendation is the newsroom's wire.
2. Contracts as graphs: term sheets, cap tables, shareholder agreements, clause to clause, versions and caveats.
3. For founders: talking to investors, next steps, services, funding models from open source to proprietary.
4. The GenAI cybersecurity startup evaluation framework (notebook-lm.sgit.ai, `2026-03-02-genai-cybersecurity-startup-evaluation`), applied in public with evidence in a vault; levels for a maturity model are new work.
5. Deal rooms as vaults (start from the Tallowmere founder's desk, investor's desk and deal room vaults).
6. Keys for investors, as a PKI demonstration.
7. sgit as the case study (strategy, marketing, thinking; not the amount raised), like The Cyber Boardroom's
   repository (link to come from Dinis).
8. RiskMandate at work: due diligence, de-risking, the insuring agents series and its vaults.

## The team
Smaller than the PHP newsroom's ("2 people and 19 agents and roles"): a conductor, a journalist, a researcher,
security and observability, on a schedule, with an append lane for messages and requests.

## Then
Dinis emails investors and asks whether it is useful and what would help; answers go on the record.
