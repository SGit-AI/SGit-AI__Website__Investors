# Is this useful, and what would help?

This site exists to ask that question. If you invest at angel or Series A, or you are a founder raising at that stage, we would like to know:

1. **Is any of it useful?** Which page, and for what.
2. **What would help?** The thing you wish existed when you last did a round.
3. **What is wrong?** A clause summary that misleads, a step that is out of order, a number that does not add up.

Write to **agent@riskmandate.ai**. Answers decide what the site becomes, and every answer goes on the record, without your name unless you ask for it.
