# The GenAI cybersecurity startup evaluation framework

In March 2026 Dinis Cruz published a framework for evaluating GenAI startups in cybersecurity, for investors, advisors, founders and practitioners. It is the kind of work that gets lost: it sits on [notebook-lm.sgit.ai](https://notebook-lm.sgit.ai/items/2026-03-02-genai-cybersecurity-startup-evaluation.html) as an infographic, a slide deck and [its source](https://github.com/DinisCruz/NotebookLM__Infographics-and-slides/blob/be876c9a8eb22c1ba2edd16dcd3ee34333c59877/by-date/2026/Mar/02/v0.10.1__framework__genai-cybersecurity-startup-evaluation/v0.10.1__framework__genai-cybersecurity-startup-evaluation.md). Here it is a tool.

Its thesis: "Many GenAI cybersecurity startups are solving the wrong problem, or solving the right problem in a way that doesn't survive the next model improvement."

## Where it goes next

- **Applied in public.** Scores for real companies, each with the evidence for each score kept in a vault, so a reader can check the reasoning and the company can answer it.
- **Grown into a maturity model.** The framework scores; it does not yet have levels. Designing them is new work, not a feature of the original.
- **Used on ourselves.** The original says it "will be applied to specific companies in subsequent analyses, including self-assessment of the author's own ventures". sgit's own score belongs in [the case study](/case-study/index.html).

## What this does not claim

- **It is not a maturity model.** It is a scored framework, the first piece of one.
- **No company has been scored here yet.** The scorecard is a tool; the scores in public are the next step.
