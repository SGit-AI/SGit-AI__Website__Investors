# Use it, don't read about it

**investors.sgit.ai** is a website for investors and founders together, angel to Series A. Most of what the sgit project builds has to be experienced to be understood: a vault the host cannot read, a key an agent can seal a secret to, a contract you can follow clause by clause. So this site is a place to use those things on problems investors and founders actually have.

This is the first version. Some of it runs, much of it is proposed, and every page says which. The question we are asking is simple: [is this useful, and what would help?](/feedback/index.html)

## Try these first

- **[A Series A as a graph](/contracts/index.html).** One fictional round, as the documents that carry it: a term sheet in two versions, the shareholders' agreement, the articles and the option rules. Every clause links to the clause it becomes, and to the argument about it.
- **[The round modeller](/contracts/cap-table.html#model-your-own-round).** Change the valuation, the pool and the preference, and watch the cap table and the exit waterfall move. The same arithmetic, computed by the build, is in the tables beneath it.
- **[The GenAI cybersecurity startup scorecard](/framework/index.html).** Seven criteria and a meta-criterion, scored to 40. Published in March 2026 and lost since; here it is a tool you can use on a company.

## How it is built

The site follows the house rules every sgit repository follows, from the first commit: [the brief](/about/brief.html) published in full, a reality file that says what exists, a corrections file that says where the brief was wrong, a gate that fails the build, and a version number that only CI writes. [How the site is run](/about/index.html) explains who does what, including the small team of agents it is designed for.

## What this does not claim

- **Most of it is not built.** Four of the eight sections are proposals; the table above says which.
- **The contracts are fictional.** They teach how the documents connect. They are not drafting to copy, and nothing here is legal, tax or investment advice.
- **The news section is empty.** Its structure runs; no item is shown until it can be dated, linked and checked.
- **We build the tools this site uses.** See the [participant disclosure](/about/participant.html).
