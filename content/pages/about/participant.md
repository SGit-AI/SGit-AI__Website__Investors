# Participant disclosure

investor.sgit.ai is published by the sgit project. The project builds the tools this site uses and describes, sgit vaults among them, and Dinis Cruz, the author of the brief, works on sgit and on RiskMandate, which the site also describes. sgit is also the site's own case study, and will at some point raise money from the kind of investor this site is written for.

So read it as a participant's site. Where a page argues for a tool we build, the argument is ours and the evidence is linked so it can be checked. Where we are not the right answer, we would rather say so.

Nothing on this site is investment, legal or tax advice. The worked contracts are fictional.
