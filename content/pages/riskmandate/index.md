# RiskMandate at work

[RiskMandate](https://riskmandate.ai/) and vaults, working on the problems founders and investors have: analysing startups, gathering material and research, market analysis, due diligence and de-risking, with the vaults that show them running.

## The proposal

- **Due diligence as a vault.** The questions, the evidence and the answers, kept together and shared by key, so the work is reusable and checkable.
- **Behaviour policies as risk.** An agent's behaviour policy can be read as an insurance policy, priced row by row and backed by capital. The [insuring agents series](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/insuring-agents-the-whole-story.html) has five articles and three vaults, including a calculator anyone can open.
- **The scorecard, with evidence.** The [evaluation framework](/framework/index.html) applied to real companies, each score backed by material in a vault.

## What this does not claim

- **Nothing on this page runs yet.** It is a proposal from [the brief](/about/brief.html). RiskMandate is a sister project; see the [participant disclosure](/about/participant.html).
