# Deal rooms as vaults

How information moves between an investor and a startup, and all the overhead around it: the deck, the model, the cap table, the contracts, the questions and the answers, and who has seen which version.

## The proposal

- **A deal room is a vault.** Encrypted in the browser, so the host stores what it cannot read. Shared by a read key, which cannot become write access.
- **Connected to an agent.** Give the deal room its own workspace, email and team accounts, and keep everything in its vault, where it is managed and protected. The agent files what arrives, answers what it can from what is there, and asks a person for the rest.
- **The record is the room.** What was shared, when, with whom, and what changed since, so neither side has to reconstruct it at completion.

## What exists to start from

The [legal on demand for startups](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/legal-on-demand-for-startups.html) article built three vaults, a founder's desk, an investor's desk and a deal room, for a fictional company. The [investor deck for sgit](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/where-to-invest-in-sgit.html) is itself published as a vault. Those are where this section starts.
